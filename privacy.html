My Personal App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

My Personal App ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal-use application that helps you work with your own Google account data. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who signs in to the App.

1. Information we access

  • Basic profile information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Google user data. Only the data covered by the permissions (scopes) you approve on the Google consent screen, and only as needed for the features you use.
  • Technical data. Basic error and usage logs needed to keep the App working.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features to you.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To fix problems and keep the App running.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

We do not share your Google user data with third parties, except when you ask us to, when needed to comply with the law, or to protect the security of the App and its users.

5. Storage, security and retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the App. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access tokens are kept private. No method of storage or transmission is fully secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. Your choices

  • You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask us to delete your data by emailing us (see below). We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
